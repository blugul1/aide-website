About Imran

The work begins with people.

Over the years I have sat across the table from business leaders, walked factory floors and sales floors with teams, trained teachers in their own classrooms, and listened to parents and young people trying to find their way. Those conversations shaped AIDE, and they still shape every session I run.

I bring twenty years of corporate and sales leadership, including fifteen years of international work across the Gulf, Europe and Africa, and a record of more than 300 workshops and 700 coaching hours. I am a psychologist working in the cognitive and behavioural fields, an NLP Master Practitioner and a hypnotherapy trained coach. Sessions are held in Urdu, English or Arabic.

Awareness changes what you see. Practice changes how you live.
Programmes

Development that reaches beyond the classroom.

Every person and every organisation starts from a different place. One needs to lead better. Another needs habits that hold, a home that breathes easier, or a child who needs direction. These programmes are built around real situations and the practice of putting new understanding to work.

Independent Initiatives

Standing alongside AIDE.

Some work is too important to sit inside one organisation. Alongside AIDE stand two independent initiatives, each with its own leadership and its own history of showing up for people. Imran serves both as an advisor.

ADK · Anjuman Dukhtaran-e-Kashmir

Halima Sadia leads ADK as CEO

Some organisations are built around an idea. This one was built around years of showing up when people needed help most. Founded in 1996 by the late Haji Mirza Khurram Haider, ADK has served needy women and families across Kashmir and Pakistan for over thirty years. In the last ten years alone it has given more than PKR 200 million in assistance to over 3,000 families, through marriages, ration, clothing, home construction and small business support. Halima Sadia leads ADK as CEO. Imran Aslam serves as its advisor and counselor.

Coaching and Personal Sessions

Sometimes you need a conversation.

Sometimes you do not need a programme. You need a conversation that helps you see your own situation clearly.

There are moments when work, family or your own mind becomes hard to navigate alone. Coaching gives you a space to say what is actually happening, understand your own responses, and leave with practical next steps.
