1:1 Coaching

One conversation, honestly held.

Private coaching for goals, habits and decisions — and counselling-informed support for relationships and family life. Online, wherever you are, or in person in Bhimber by arrangement.

1:1 coaching at AIDE is structured, confidential work on behaviour — habits, decisions, relationships, leadership — with a psychologist and NLP Master Practitioner. It is not therapy and does not diagnose or treat mental illness; where clinical help is needed, you will be referred honestly. Sessions run online or in Bhimber, in Urdu, English or Arabic.

Most people arrive with a tangle: a habit that will not hold, a decision that will not settle, a relationship pattern that keeps repeating. The work is to untangle it in the right order — first clarity about what is actually happening, then a small number of practices that fit a real life, then honest review.

Sessions run in Urdu, English or Arabic, and the first step is never a commitment: a free fifteen-minute discovery call decides together whether coaching, a programme, or something else entirely is the honest fit.

Not sure where you stand? The sixty-second self-check on the home page is a fair starting mirror.

How it works

Three steps, no surprises.

1

Free discovery call

Fifteen minutes on WhatsApp. You say briefly what you want to work on; we decide together if this is the right room for it.

2

A clear agreement

Format, language, schedule and fee are clarified in writing before any session is confirmed. Nothing is charged before you have the full picture. A single session is PKR 7,500, stated openly — PKR 6,000 during the opening offer (14 November to 31 December 2026); a four-session entry package is PKR 26,000. Longer packages are agreed in conversation.

3

Sessions, with practice

Each session ends with a small, concrete practice for the week. Progress is reviewed honestly — including when the honest answer is to stop or change direction.

Relationships & family

A large part of this practice is couples and families — the work that also runs as Strong Homes and the Love Clinic conversations. The philosophy is simple: no villain, no victim. Not the husband as tyrant, not the wife as accused, not the in-laws as the enemy. Responsibility, dignity and dialogue — practised, not preached.

Where a situation needs a clinician, a qualified lawyer, or a scholar, that is said plainly and you are pointed the right way. A first session gives clarity; it does not deliver a verdict on anyone.

The boundary, stated plainly: coaching is not a substitute for medical or psychological treatment. Where clinical care is what you need, you will be told so and referred appropriately — that honesty is part of the service, not a footnote to it.
