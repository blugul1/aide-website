Store policies

Delivery & access.

What happens between your payment and the book in your hand — step by step, with nothing tucked away.

What you receive

Each book arrives as a print PDF, a mobile PDF made for reading on a phone, an EPUB for reader apps, and a DOCX — plus its free workbook. Your copy carries a small watermark with your name, your order number and the purchase date — the purchase terms explain why.

How it reaches you

Once your payment is verified, your book is sent to you on WhatsApp and by email — the two addresses you gave when you ordered. Keep the email safe; it is your permanent copy.

How long verification takes

Every payment is checked against the actual account before anything is approved. That care protects you as much as it protects us — a screenshot alone can be wrong, or worse. Payment verification usually takes a few hours; on a busy day it can run to the next working day. Meanwhile the free sample chapter of your book is open to you right here on the site, so your reading does not have to wait.

New phone? Lost the file?

Message us on WhatsApp (+92 330 9222029) with your order number and the name you ordered under. We check the order and send your copy again — your purchase does not expire with your phone.

If something goes wrong

A file that will not open, a broken link, the wrong book in your inbox — same WhatsApp, same order number, and we will put it right. The money side of things lives on the refund & replacement page.
