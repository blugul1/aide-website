Store policies

Privacy.

The short version: we keep as little as we can, use it only for what you asked for, and never sell it.

Browsing this site

This site is a set of static pages. It carries no advertising trackers, and nobody builds a profile of you here. Your browser may keep small local data on your own device for features you use — nothing more.

Assessments

Your answers and results in the Assessment Centre stay in your own browser. They are not sent to us, and you can delete them yourself at any time from your dashboard there.

When you buy a book

We keep your name, WhatsApp number and email for three things only: delivering your book, helping you if something goes wrong, and keeping the order record. That is all. Your details are never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never put on a marketing list unless you ask to be on one. Order records are kept as ordinary business records.

When you message us

WhatsApp conversations run on WhatsApp's own platform, under its privacy terms. What you share in coaching stays confidential, as the coaching pages say.
