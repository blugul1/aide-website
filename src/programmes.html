Programmes

Six ways in.

Signature programmes built from real practice, not theory. Admissions open in cohorts — fees and dates are confirmed on the free discovery call, in writing, before anything is charged.

AIDE's six signature programmes are structured courses with fixed formats, live sessions and field practice — leadership on the Prophetic model (USWA), habit change, behavioural sales, teacher emotional intelligence, strong homes and youth life design. Each is built from real practice in Pakistan and the Gulf, taught in Urdu, English or Arabic, and measured by behaviour change, not applause.
