About

Zill-e-Huma brings NLP, life coaching, hypnotherapy and training together in one calm practice. Her work reaches the quiet layers where patterns are formed, and helps people set down old ones and step forward with a lighter heart.

Her qualifications sit in two worlds: the classroom — an MPhil in Botany and an MA in Teacher Education, with years of science teaching in Bhimber — and the practice room, where NLP, coaching and hypnotherapy meet the patterns that run a life from underneath.
